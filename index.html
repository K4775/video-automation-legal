Video Automation

A personal tool by Kilian

Video Automation automatically creates short vertical videos from text, images and music and uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works

  • The creator connects their TikTok account once via TikTok's official login.
  • The tool generates a short video (title, description, background, optional music).
  • The video is uploaded to the connected account, where the creator reviews and posts it.
