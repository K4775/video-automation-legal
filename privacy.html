Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Automation (the "App"), operated by Kilian, handles data when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok account information provided through TikTok Login Kit (e.g. open ID, display name, avatar), only as permitted by the scopes you approve.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used solely to upload videos on your behalf.
  • Videos and captions that the App creates and uploads to your account.

2. How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate with TikTok and to upload videos to the connected account. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the device that runs the App and are never published or shared. Generated videos are stored locally until you delete them.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to TikTok via its official API in order to perform uploads you requested.

5. Retention and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok settings under "Security and permissions". Locally stored tokens and videos can be deleted at any time; on request we delete all data related to your account.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data of users below the minimum age required by TikTok.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this URL.
